01 / Premise

Cognition is becoming something people can measure, see, and act on.

Consumer neurotechnology and physiological sensing are moving cognitive data beyond specialist laboratories. Our work asks what these measurements mean to people, how interfaces shape their interpretation, and how we can design technologies that support agency rather than obscure it.

02 / Research directions

Between measure and meaning

01

Sensing the mind

Exploring consumer neurotechnology, physiological sensing, and the limits of what everyday devices can claim to know.

02

Making sense of cognition

Studying interpretation, uncertainty, reflection, and the stories people build around cognitive measurements.

03

Designing future interfaces

Creating embodied, interactive, and responsible systems that make complex cognitive data understandable and useful.

03 / Collaboration

Built across disciplines and institutions.

Cognitive Futures Lab brings together perspectives from human-computer interaction, cognitive psychology, consumer neurotechnology, data sensemaking, and mechanical engineering.

We are developing this work with collaborators at TU Delft, Eindhoven University of Technology, and the University of Twente.
