Research

Between measure and meaning.

We study what happens when cognitive and physiological measurements move into everyday life: how claims are made, how people interpret them, and how interactive technologies can support more careful forms of sensemaking.

01 / Directions

01

Sensing the mind

We investigate consumer neurotechnology and physiological sensing without treating measurements as transparent representations of mental states. What is sensed, what is inferred, and what is communicated are distinct questions.

02

Making sense of cognition

We examine how people interpret cognitive data, live with uncertainty, and develop stories about attention, stress, performance, and selfhood.

03

Human–technology interaction

We study how interfaces, feedback, and interaction shape what cognitive measurements appear to mean—and how those meanings influence behaviour and self-understanding.

04

Future interfaces

We prototype embodied and interactive systems that expose uncertainty, invite reflection, and support responsible engagement with cognitive technologies.

02 / Approach

Interdisciplinary by design.

Our research combines methods from human-computer interaction, cognitive psychology, design research, data sensemaking, and engineering. Depending on the question, projects may involve controlled studies, qualitative inquiry, participatory workshops, interface design, prototyping, or critical and speculative approaches.

Projects and publications will be added here as the lab’s work develops.
