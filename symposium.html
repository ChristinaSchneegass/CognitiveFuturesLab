02 / Who should join

Open to intersecting domains.

We welcome researchers at every career stage whose work touches cognitive psychology, neuroscience, HCI, design, data visualisation and sensemaking, ethics, responsible innovation, wearable computing, biosensing, mechanical engineering, or adjacent areas.

We also invite companies developing consumer neurotechnology, physiological sensing, wearables, research hardware, cognitive-data tools, and interfaces that help people interpret complex measurements.
